# Third-party notices for `ksj-viewer`

## arrShow display semantics

`ksj-viewer` contains an app-local C++/Qt port of the bounded display
semantics used by [arrShow](https://github.com/tsumpf/arrShow), including its
complex-component vocabulary and `complex2rgb` / `martin_phase` colour
behaviour. KSpaceJet does not distribute MATLAB, arrShow MATLAB source files,
or arrShow data assets, and does not implement arrShow workspace or
destructive-array operations.
